Examination slots, bookings and results in one place

The Admin sets up an examination and its timeline, examiners publish the slots they can take, students book a seat, and marks are awarded against a rubric.

Log in Register as a student Register as an examiner

  • AdminManages courses, examinations, rubrics and examiners, opens and closes each phase, and can reschedule a booking or change a slot's examiner.
  • ExaminerCreates slots during the slot creation period, sees who has booked, and evaluates each student using the rubric. Needs Admin approval first.
  • StudentSearches examinations, books one slot per examination during the booking period, and views published results and feedback.
